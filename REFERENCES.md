# Model art direction — After Hours

## Comparison guiding this revision
The first version arranged box-shaped buildings behind a conventional heading, navigation, and HTML portfolio panels. Jesse's Ramen instead makes a freestanding authored model the entire screen, introduces it through a loader and START button, and lets the camera orbit and enter physical objects.

This phase replaces the page shell with the requested loading sequence and a full-screen, orbitable model. Portfolio sections are deferred until the model has been reviewed.

## Original scene design
A compact restaurant and netrunner workshop beneath a narrow hotel. Large primary forms establish the silhouette; inset shop interiors, balconies, utilities, service stairs, and rooftop machinery add depth. A magenta hotel sign and amber restaurant frontage anchor the image. Cyan workshop and hologram details provide contrast. A coupe, scooter, stools, bowls, bottles, pedestrians, and an umbrella establish scale.

All geometry, sign graphics, and material textures in this revision are original and generated locally in code. No paid or externally licensed models are embedded.
